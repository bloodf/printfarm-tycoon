# M1.1 change request: "Look & Feel" + Point & Click

**From the director (M1 gate review):**
> "The assets still look plastic and made by AI. We need a better aesthetic and improved textures.
> Also we need a way to play just point and click."

**Decision:** M1 is *approved with changes*. M2 waits until M1.1 passes its own gate.

---

## 1. Diagnosis: why M1 reads as "plastic / CG"

| # | Cause | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Flat ambient light.** A uniform hemisphere light and environment light everywhere. No bounce light, no colour bleeding, no darker corners | Walls and ceiling the same value everywhere; objects don't sit *in* the room |
| 2 | **No ambient occlusion in the assets.** Crevices, undersides and contact areas are as bright as open surfaces (screen-space AO only helps a little) | Printers look like toys; props float |
| 3 | **Materials too uniform.** One roughness value per material, no smudges, wear, dust or grime | "Showroom plastic" |
| 4 | **Textures too simple.** Soft FFT noise, 1K, obvious tiling, no macro variation; the block wall is too perfect | Floor and walls look like wallpaper |
| 5 | **A sterile, empty set.** Real garages are lived-in: cables, clutter, tools, OSB panels, mats, paint cans | "Rendered room", not a place |
| 6 | **Colour grade.** ACES plus a saturation boost gives candy colours; no film response (grain, soft highlights) | Toy-like colours |
| 7 | **Perfect geometry.** Everything straight and aligned, cables only where modelled, no labels or stickers on the machines | CG precision |

## 2. Approach (how AAA interiors get their realism)

1. **Baked global illumination for the room.** Blender Cycles bakes *indirect* light (bounces, colour bleeding, contact
   occlusion from the furniture) into lightmaps for the floor, walls, ceiling and door. Direct light stays real-time,
   so the printers and moving objects still cast live shadows (the same approach as Unity's "Baked Indirect" mode).
2. **Baked ambient occlusion on every asset.** Each printer, prop and box gets an AO texture on a second UV set
   (native glTF occlusion, so three.js uses it automatically).
3. **Texture set v2 (2K, layered).** A polished concrete slab (aggregate, trowel swirls, hairline cracks, oil and tyre
   stains), painted block with roller stipple and scuffs, **OSB panels**, plywood, real butcher block (strips, grain, scratches),
   scratched metals, powder-coat orange peel, injection-moulded plastic texture, rubber, better cardboard.
4. **Engine "weathering" shader** on every material: world-space breakup of roughness and albedo (smudges,
   fingerprints, dust), grime near the floor on walls and furniture legs, and dirt that collects where the AO is dark.
5. **Lived-in set dressing:** OSB wall panels, a rolling tool chest, a wall shelf with paint cans and spray bottles, an
   anti-fatigue mat, a rug under the desk, a broom, a ladder, stacked boxes, extension cords on the floor, printer power cables
   running to the power strip, clutter on the bench (scraper, calipers, glue stick, filament offcuts, failed prints).
6. **Machine details:** warning stickers, serial plates, cable sleeves, scuffs on the PEI plates.
7. **Lighting and grade:** physically-based intensities, practical lights (desk lamp, P1S chamber light, bench LED strip),
   AgX tone mapping, a softer grade, subtle film grain, depth of field when you focus on a UI.

## 3. Point & Click mode (CONTROLS brief §Point & Click)

- **A second control scheme**, chosen on New Game, in Settings, or with a HUD button: `First-person` | `Point & Click`.
- **Camera:** a smooth orbit, pan and zoom camera kept inside the garage. Right-drag rotates, drag with the middle
  button or Shift pans, the wheel zooms, double-click on the floor re-centres. Clicking an object glides the camera to frame it.
- **Interaction:** hover → outline + a tooltip at the cursor. Click → an **action popover** with one button per action
  (the same actions as E/F in first person). "Hold" actions become timed buttons with a progress ring.
- **Hands:** the held-item card gets buttons (Drop, Put back, Throw away). Clicking a target with something in your
  hands offers the matching action (e.g. holding a spool → click a printer → "Load spool").
- **HUD toolbar** (both modes): Laptop, time controls (▶ / ▶▶), camera reset, mode switch, menu.
- **Fully mouse-only:** every action in the game is reachable without a keyboard. Tutorial hints adapt to the mode.

## 4. Acceptance criteria (M1.1 gate)
- [ ] Before/after comparison from the same camera views (`docs/qa/before_after/`).
- [ ] Every GLB has baked AO; the garage shell has baked indirect lightmaps.
- [ ] At least 8 new set-dressing props; printers have decals and cables.
- [ ] A complete day can be played **mouse-only** in Point & Click mode (automated playthrough with zero key presses).
- [ ] The first-person playthrough and the unit tests still pass.
- [ ] Zip delivered without `node_modules` and `dist`.
